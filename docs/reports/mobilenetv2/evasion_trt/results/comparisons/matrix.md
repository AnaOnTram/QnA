## Limitations

- 100 stratified Imagenette-320 validation images over 10 ImageNet classes (full 1000-class head); one sample seed and per-epsilon attack seeds.
- Finite FGSM/PGD/Square attacks are not certificates of robustness.
- TensorRT runtimes are forward-only; PGD gradients come from a differentiable FP32 replica (BPDA with straight-through estimators for quantized arms).
- Success denominators are explicit: own clean-correct and all-arm shared clean-correct; the two are never mixed.
